Battle Baseball brings the format, the app, the radar, and the families. Your team brings volunteers, and keeps a share of every player's registration.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your team below.
  2. We pick a date and a field together and set your team's share.
  3. Your volunteers serve as coaches, scorekeepers, and coordinators on the day. The app walks them through check-in, the radar, the scorebook, and awards.
  4. After the event, we send your team its share and you see the total raised.

What Battle Baseball provides

  • Registration, payments, waivers, and reminders in the app
  • Radar and the event-day tools
  • Promotion on Facebook and the website
  • Results, leaderboards, and player cards families share

What your team provides

  • Volunteers who have passed your league's background check
  • Help with a field, if you have one
  • Spreading the word to families in your area
