Assumption of risk

I understand that baseball involves risks, including injury from thrown or batted balls, collisions, and falls. I accept these risks on behalf of the player I register.

Release

To the extent the law allows, I release Battle Baseball, its organizers, host teams, and volunteers from claims arising from the player's participation, except for gross negligence or willful misconduct.

Medical release

I authorize event staff to seek emergency medical treatment for the player if I can't be reached, and I'm responsible for its cost. I've given an emergency contact in the app.

Photos

Photos and video may be taken at events. Players with private profiles won't be named in anything we publish.
