Four steps

How a Showcase works

Not a team tournament: every player signs up on their own.

  • 1. Register

    Add your player once in the app, then pick an age group and skill level. Returning families are done in under two minutes.

  • 2. Reserve

    Send the fee by Venmo or Cash App with a short code in the note, then tap Reserve. We hold the spot while we confirm it.

  • 3. Play

    Players are placed by skill level, then teams are evened out by hand. Radar on every fastball; opt-in skills contests at some events.

  • 4. Share

    Velocities, stats, and awards post to the app and the leaderboards. Parents decide whether a profile is public.

What we measure

Numbers that mean something

Every number on a leaderboard was taken by staff at a Battle Baseball Showcase. Self-reported numbers are used for placement only and never shown.

  • MPHFastball velocity, on radar, at every event.
  • HR · WHome runs and team wins, tracked game by game.
  • AwardsMVP, Top Pitcher, Top Hitter, Elite Defender, and more in every division.

Some events add an opt-in skills contest: catcher pop time and the 40-yard dash.

Questions

Who can play?

Youth players of any skill level. Players are placed by age group and skill level so everyone plays with kids at their level. Age groups for the first event are coming soon.

How do I pay?

By Venmo or Cash App. The app shows the handle and a short code to put in the payment note. Tap Reserve after you pay and we'll hold the spot while we confirm it, usually within a few hours.

What if it rains?

If rain cancels a showcase, you pick a full refund or a credit toward the next one. Families who cancel more than a week ahead get a full refund.

Is my child's information public?

Only if you say so. Profiles are private by default. You choose whether your player appears on results and leaderboards, and whether it shows their full name or just first name and last initial. Birth dates, schools, and contacts are never public.

Where is the app?

Battle Baseball works in your browser today at showcase.getbattleplan.app/app. iPhone and Android apps are on the way.
