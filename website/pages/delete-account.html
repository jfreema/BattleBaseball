Delete your account

In the app or on this site, sign in, open Account, and tap Delete account. It deletes your account, your players, their private details, and their player cards right away.

Can't sign in? Email support@getbattleplan.app from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll delete your account, your players, and their private details within 30 days.

Payment records an admin marked (the amount, the payment code, and the app it came from) may be kept as long as tax rules require. Published results stay on past events without your player's name.
