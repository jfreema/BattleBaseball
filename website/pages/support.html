How can we help?

Email support@getbattleplan.app and we'll get back to you, usually within a day.

My spot says Confirming payment

That means we haven't matched a Venmo or Cash App payment to your spot yet. Check that it went to the account shown when you reserved, with your code (like BB-7K2F) in the note. If your spot changes to Still matching, tap I already paid and an admin will look for it first.

Refunds

Cancel more than a week before a showcase for a full refund. If rain cancels a showcase, you pick a refund or a credit toward the next one. Refunds go back the way you paid.

Player privacy

A player's card is private unless you switch on Public card (Players, your player, then Edit). You choose full name or first name and last initial. Birth dates, schools, and contacts are never public.
